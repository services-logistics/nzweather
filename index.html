Privacy Policy

Updated on December 28, 2025.

Introduction

NZ Weather, an application developed by Logistics Oasis Limited ("we", "our", or "us"), is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use NZ Weather (the "App").

This policy applies to users worldwide, including users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), and New Zealand. We comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR, and New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Information We Collect

NZ Weather handles personal and sensitive user data (location) in accordance with the Google Play User Data Policy, GDPR, and UK GDPR.

Location Data

We request access to your device's location to automatically detect your nearest New Zealand city and provide accurate local weather forecasts.

How We Use Location Data:

  • Location data we collect through this app is only used to determine your nearest city from our pre-defined list of New Zealand locations.
  • The location is processed locally on your device.
  • We do not track your location movements or history.
  • We do not store your precise location data on our servers.
  • Third-party advertising partners (AdMob) may use your IP address to find an approximate location as described in their policies. Precise location information is not provided to advertising partners.

Your Control:

  • Granting this app location permission is optional.
  • If you deny location permission, the app defaults to showing the weather forecasts for the default New Zealand location of Auckland.
  • You can manually select any New Zealand city from the dropdown menu at any time.
  • You can revoke location permissions through your device's settings.

Device and Technical Information

The App collects the following technical information:

  • Device Identifiers: Device ID and package name for security verification purposes.
  • App Usage Data: Launch count (stored locally) to determine when to prompt for in-app reviews.
  • Cached Weather Data: Weather information temporarily stored on your device for up to 15 minutes to reduce network usage and improve performance.
  • Security Tokens: Google Play Integrity tokens generated when fetching weather data to verify the authenticity of app requests (Android only).

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR/UK GDPR):

  • Consent: Location data processing is based on your explicit consent, which you provide when granting location permissions.
  • Legitimate Interests: Device and technical information is processed based on our legitimate interest in providing a secure, functional, and optimized weather service.
  • Performance of Contract: Weather data caching is necessary to provide you with the weather forecast service you've requested.

Third-Party Services

The App integrates third-party SDKs and services that may process limited data necessary for functionality, security, analytics, and advertising.

Google AdMob

Our App displays advertisements through Google AdMob. For users in the EEA and UK, we request and display non-personalized ads by default in compliance with GDPR. We implement Google's Consent Management Platform to obtain your consent before serving personalized ads.

Information that AdMob may collect:

  • Device ID and advertising ID
  • Device information (model and operating system)
  • Ad interaction data
  • Basic location information (country/regional level based on IP address)
  • IP address

For EEA/UK users: When you first launch the app, you will be presented with a consent dialog allowing you to choose whether to consent to personalized advertising. You can change your consent preferences at any time through the "Privacy Settings" option in the app.

For more information about Google's advertising practices, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Help

Google Play Integrity API

We use Google Play Integrity API to verify that weather data requests are coming from genuine, unmodified versions of our app. This security measure helps protect our backend services from abuse and ensures data integrity.

  • Integrity tokens are generated on-demand when fetching weather data<./li>
  • Tokens contain information about your device's security status and app authenticity.
  • No personal information is included in these tokens.
  • Tokens are transmitted to our backend for verification only.

React Native and Associated Libraries

Our App uses these React Native and related libraries for the weather app's functionality:

  • expo-location (for location detection)
  • react-native-google-mobile-ads (for advertisements and consent management)
  • react-native-svg (for weather graphics and charts)
  • react-native-mmkv (for fast local data storage)
  • @react-native-async-storage/async-storage (fallback storage)
  • react-native-google-play-integrity (security verification)
  • react-native-in-app-review (optional app review prompts)

These libraries operate locally on your device and do not transmit personal information to external servers beyond what is documented in this privacy policy.

Data Storage and Security

  • The Application does not maintain any user accounts or profiles.
  • No personal information is transmitted to, or stored on, our servers.
  • Weather data is temporarily cached on your device using encrypted local storage for display purposes only.
  • Cached weather data is automatically cleaned up after 7 days to minimize storage usage.
  • Your selected city preference is stored locally on your device.
  • All data processing occurs locally on your device except for generalised weather data requests to our backend service. This service retrieves weather data for the whole of New Zealand and is then processed on device.
  • We implement industry-standard security measures including API authentication and integrity verification to protect security.

Data Retention

  • Location Data: Not retained. Location is used momentarily to determine your nearest city and is immediately discarded.
  • Weather Cache: Stored on your device for up to 15 minutes for performance optimisation, with automatic cleanup of weather data older than 7 days.
  • City Selection: Stored locally on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data.
  • Launch Count: Stored locally on your device for review prompt timing only.
  • No historical data is stored on our servers.

Your Rights and Choices

Rights for All Users

You have the right to:

  • Deny location permissions (the Application will still work with manual city selection)
  • Revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings
  • Uninstall the App to remove all locally cached data
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns or questions

Additional Rights for EEA and UK Users (GDPR/UK GDPR).

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR, you have some additional rights:

  • Right of Access: Request information about what personal data we process.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data (uninstalling the app removes all local data).
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request limitation of how we process your data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your personal data in a structured format.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for location access or ad personalization at any time.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Since our app processes minimal data locally and doesn't maintain server-side user accounts, most of these rights can be exercised directly through your device settings or by uninstalling the app. For any questions about exercising your rights, please contact us.
